## 1. Title, hook and back-cover copy

**Final title:** *The Map That Forgot Home*

**One-sentence hook:** When the roads on an old lighthouse map start to vanish, ten-year-old Mara has seventy-two hours to cross a hidden island world and stop a man who wants to fold her whole town into paper for ever.

**Back-cover description (≈150 words):**

> Four friends. Three days. A way home that is disappearing.
>
> Mara Ellery likes plans, lists and maps that tell the truth. So when the map she finds in the Gull Head lighthouse starts growing islands, and the road out of Wrenmouth turns grey and then vanishes into fog, she knows something is badly wrong.
>
> Beyond the lighthouse door lies the Fold: lamplit harbours, a forest that steals your voice, an orchard that hangs upside down and a market that never stays in the same place twice. Somewhere at its edge, the Surveyor has started the Stilling. In seventy-two hours every island on his great Chart will freeze for ever, and that includes Mara's town, her dad and her nan.
>
> With a boy who fixes things too fast, a girl who notices everything but rarely says so, and a Fold-born storyteller with a lantern full of echoes, Mara must find the one kind of road the Surveyor can't control.

### Market positioning (business development note)

- **Shelf:** illustrated middle-grade adventure (8–12), ~28,000 words, front map plus 12 black-and-white interiors. That length and format suit independent readers in Years 4–7 and work as a class read-aloud.
- **What sells it:** a usable map that readers consult, fair puzzles they can solve first, and a clock on the cover ("72 hours").
- **Schools angle:** map-reading, compass points, scale and journey times tie into KS2 geography. The back matter makes a ready-made activity pack (code puzzle, route puzzle, design-your-own-island).
- **Series potential:** the Fold keeps changing after Book 1, so new islands can appear. The final page hints at a return with no retcon.
- **Extras for publishers and booksellers:** a front-map poster, a "design an island" bookshop or school competition, and a printable equipment card set.

## 2. Character guide

### Mara Ellery, 10 (viewpoint character)
- **Look:** shoulder-length wavy chestnut hair, freckles, bright eyes; tan canvas jacket over a cream top; brown backpack; always holding a sketchbook with a pencil.
- **Home:** lives over Ellery's Fish and Chips on Wrenmouth harbour with **Dad (Tom)**, who runs the shop, and **Nan (Dorothy "Dot" Ellery, 78)**, who moved in last April and is losing her memory. **Mum (Shona)** works two-weeks-on, two-weeks-off on an offshore wind-farm crew and is away this fortnight.
- **Motivation:** keep Nan, Dad and Wrenmouth from being stilled. Underneath that, she wants the world to stay the shape she has drawn it.
- **Ability:** spatial memory and sketching. She notices patterns on maps and on the ground, counts steps and reads routes.
- **Limitation:** needs the perfect plan before she moves. She is so afraid of making a mistake that hurts someone that she freezes or over-controls, and she doesn't listen well when she's sure.
- **Early mistake (Ch4):** insists on detouring to the Upside-Down Orchard "to be certain" rather than heading straight for the Observatory. It costs the crew a day, though it also gains them the Old Piles and the Night Market.
- **Painful mistake (Ch16):** misreads Edie's clue ("he only holds what *he* has drawn") and inks their secret raft-way onto the map to make it steady. The Surveyor erases it and the Glassers nearly drown.
- **Vulnerability:** (Ch18) admits she half-wished Nan could stay exactly as she is, which is what the Surveyor is offering.
- **Indispensable contribution:** reads the Great Chart from inside the Tower, signals the moving way and refuses to draw the true route. She chooses change over stillness.
- **Growth:** from "uncertainty means failure" to "a living way moves; that's what keeps it alive." She learns to listen to Bea, to Nan and to the plan's weak points.

## 3. The world's essential rules

### The Fold
- A hidden archipelago lying in the creases beside our world. Its islands float on **the Slack**, a flat, silver, misty sea that **breathes**: high breath at **7 a.m. and 7 p.m.**, low breath at **1 a.m. and 1 p.m.**
- For about **an hour either side of low breath**, the Slack lies almost still. This is **the Hush**. Outside the Hush, currents run strongly.
- The whole Fold is ringed by **the Rim**, a wall of mist. Travellers who walk into it come out facing the way they came.
- **Time** in the Fold runs at the same rate as ours, except for the Stilling's slowing effect (below).

### Routes ("ways")
1. A **way** is any route between two islands that people actually travel: bridge, causeway, ferry line, cable or path. A way is **living** once it has been travelled end to end and still holds.
2. Ways hold islands near one another. Without them, islands drift very slowly.
3. **Recorded ways** are drawn in **tide-ink** on the **Great Chart** in the Surveyor's Tower. They are steady and don't drift.
4. **Unrecorded ways** (Glasser reed paths, the Night Market's boat crossings, any new path) are invisible to the Chart. Across open Slack they drift unless physically held, by rope, stakes, boats or hands.

### Tide-ink and the Great Chart
- **Ink only sets on a true line.** Tide-ink drawn along a way exactly where it runs sets and records it. Drawn anywhere else, it beads up and runs off. A way that keeps moving can't be inked true.
- **Recording needs sight or exact knowledge.** The Surveyor must see a way, or know exactly where it runs, to draw it true.
- **Erasing:** whoever keeps the Chart can scrape a recorded way off it. The real way then **fades over one hour**, from colour to grey to nothing, and is **gone for good**. Bridges crumble into the Slack and causeway stones sink. Erased ways do not come back, even after the Chart is broken.
- **Outside the Fold**, his ink is weaker. He can't unmake Wrenmouth's roads, only fold them behind a wall of Rim-mist (the fog in Chapters 1–2). That mist lifts when the Chart loses its hold.
- **Travellers on recorded ways** show on the Chart, and on any true copy, as tiny moving specks. Travellers on unrecorded ways **don't show**.

### Copy maps (Mara's map)
- Edie's map is a **true copy**. It mirrors the Great Chart: routes, specks, the border marks and fading.
- **Tide-ink drawn on a copy travels to the Chart.** Pencil and ordinary ink do nothing. Edie's old black-ink drawing of Wrenmouth never changes.
- Edie **cut her map loose** by letting it stay blank, since a blank copy can't be read from the other end. When the Surveyor drew Edie's Lamp Road onto his Chart, her map was **re-joined through the shared road** and began to fill. That's why islands "appear" in Chapter 1.
- **Edie's door:** while both lamps of the Lamp Road are lit, the Gull Head lamp-room door opens onto the road of light for whoever holds the map, and anyone touching them. The same works in reverse at the Great Lamp on Lantern Quay. **No other doorways work this way.**

### The Lamp Road
- A way made of light between **two lamps**: the Great Lamp on Lantern Quay and the old paraffin lamp at Gull Head lighthouse. A **15-minute walk**.
- Edie made it about 45 years ago and **never recorded it**. For decades it was the one way the Surveyor couldn't touch, and while it existed undrawn, **he couldn't begin the Stilling.**
- This summer his Chainmen found it (and surveyed Wrenmouth, so the town's "council men with brass chains" in August were Chainmen). He recorded it. **A recorded way must end somewhere on the Chart**, so he drew Wrenmouth in. He also believes his old friend Edie still lives there and wants her "safe".

### The Stilling: the 72-hour countdown
- **Began:** Friday, 3:00 p.m. **Ends:** Monday, 3:00 p.m.
- The Great Chart's border has **72 notches**. Each hour, on the hour, one notch is sealed into a solid line. On copies, one border mark **fades** each hour. Mara watches the first go at 4:00 p.m. on Friday.
- At the **72nd hour**, the border closes. Every inhabited island inside it, joined by recorded ways, becomes **Still**: people, birds, water, fixed in one moment inside the Chart, unchanging and unreachable, for ever.
- **Nobody can stop the notches.** Not the Surveyor, not a spell, not breaking a pen. The only failure point is the final stroke (below).
- **Slowing:** a place newly drawn into the Chart sinks in "like a stone in honey". From **Friday 5:00 p.m.** (when Wrenmouth's drawing is complete), Wrenmouth runs at about **one minute per Fold hour**. The Fold islands have always been on the Chart and run normally until the end. *Consequence:* Wrenmouth experiences ~70 minutes while the crew spend ~70 hours in the Fold.
- **Intermediate consequences:** the Surveyor posts a **Closure Schedule** ("For the safety of all: be at home") and erases ways in order, closest to his Tower first, so nobody is caught "between places" at the end. Communities are cut off one by one.

| Glasswater Causeway (Clapper's Landing–Heron Holm) | Sat 1 p.m. | 12–1 p.m. |

He also **physically destroys** unrecorded ways he finds. Chainmen burn Glasser reed paths and hunt the Night Market.

### The Surveyor and his weakness
- **Who:** Ansel Crane, about 70. Tall, stooped, ink-stained fingers, spectacles on a chain, a soft and exact voice. When he was twelve his little brother **Pip** set off along a drifting, unrecorded way to see a "new island" and was never found. Ansel has spent his life making the Fold safe from change.
- **Motive (understandable, not acceptable):** "Change takes people. Travel loses them. Still, everyone will be home, and no one will ever be lost again."
- **Power, shown through consequences:** a causeway sinks, a bridge full of bells falls into the Slack, the Glassers' homes burn, the Market scatters, Pell's family loses their home.
- **Weakness:** he controls **only ways recorded on his Chart**. He can't see, close or track an unrecorded way.
- **Boundaries:** he **can** record an unrecorded way if he sees it or knows exactly where it runs, and then erase it. Chainmen **can** destroy one physically. He has the **Long Glass** (a great telescope) to sweep the Fold. So a hidden way must stay **unseen, undrawable and unbroken**.
- **Hesper's Rule** (plaque in the Observatory, found Ch15). Its **last line** is the hinge of the book:
  1. What is drawn true in tide-ink, the Chart shall hold.
  2. What the Chart holds, its keeper may loose.
  3. No ink shall set on a way that is not where it is drawn.
  4. A border closed shall still all that lies within.
  5. **But the border shall not close while any living way between the islands lies undrawn; and a border that fails to close is broken, and may never be drawn again.**
- **Why he overlooks it (character flaw):** he trusts his system. He has burned every reed path and believes the Market's boats aren't "ways" and Hesper's Old Piles aren't a way because nobody ever walked them. He watches his Chart and his Long Glass, never the dark directly beneath his own Tower.
- **Clues before the climax:** see the weakness clue tracker in section 6.

## 6. Twenty-four-chapter outline

*Clock times are Fold time. "Left" = time remaining when the chapter opens.*

**PART ONE: THE ROAD OF LIGHT**

**Ch1. Islands That Weren't There.** *Gull Head lighthouse, Wrenmouth · Fri 3:40–4:05 p.m. · Left: 71 h 20 m*
- **Event:** Mara, mapping the lighthouse, opens Edie's chart drawer: the map, the thread and a dry pen. Islands fill the blank sea, a "Lamp Road" inks itself in, and the first border mark fades at 4:00. The Wren Road greys.
- **Clue / payoff:** two inks (Edie's black stays still); the pencil test; the water-stained margin note; Chainmen "council men" memory; Nan's sticky-note maps.
- **Relationships:** Mara and Idris snipe ("Clipboard"); Bea appears, quiet.
- **Hook:** the fog on Wren Bridge is exactly the shape of the grey road.

**Ch2. Nan's Way Home.** *Wrenmouth harbour and lighthouse · Fri 4:05–5:00 p.m. · Left: 70 h 55 m*
- **Event:** Nan is missing. Mara finds her using Edie's *old* Wrenmouth (the Palace dance hall). Nan reads the margin note by tilting it to the light. Fog closes every road out. Dad can't listen. Mara leaves a note, returns at dusk, and finds Idris has lit the lamp. The door opens onto light.
- **Clue / payoff:** Edie's note ("he only holds what he has drawn"); Nan: "never draw a line all the way round, leave a gap for the wanderers"; gulls slowing.
- **Relationships:** Mara and Nan, tender; Bea's quiet warning nearly missed.
- **Hook:** Mara steps onto the road of light, Idris follows, and Bea takes her hand. Behind them a gull hangs in the air. **70 hours remaining.**

**Ch3. The Lamp Road.** *Lamp Road and Lantern Quay · Fri 5:00–7:05 p.m. · Left: 70 h*
- **Event:** the walk on light; three specks on the map (their own); chips shared. At Lantern Quay, Tavi challenges them, then sees Edie's map. Ro confirms the Stilling, the slowing and why Wrenmouth is included, and tells them to go home. Mara refuses.
- **Clue / payoff:** recorded ways show travellers; "a recorded road must end somewhere on the Chart"; the Surveyor's notice "BE AT HOME".
- **Relationships:** Tavi and Idris rivalry; Tavi listens to Bea.
- **Hook:** Bellbridge tolls the lost-road toll. The Observatory Causeway vanishes from the map. Ro reaches for Edie's last letter: "Tavi, fetch your lantern."

**Ch4. What the Lantern Heard.** *Lantern Quay · Fri 7:05 p.m.–Sat 5:45 a.m. · Left: 68 h*
- **Event:** the echo lantern on Edie's letter gives Edie's voice: "...the answer's where he and I learned our letters. Under the water, behind the stars." Ro explains the Lamp Road history, the rules of tide-ink and copies, the Closure Schedule and why she can't leave (the Great Lamp). The plan argument: Idris wants the direct route to the Flooded Observatory, but Mara insists on the Orchard to ask Amma Oduya first and be *certain*. Tavi backs Mara (secretly for news of his parents); Bea stays silent. Idris mends Ro's teapot with thread and Mara snaps.
- **Clue / payoff:** a boat's speck vanishes as it leaves the ferry lane (**weakness clue**); Ro's proverb "It's always darkest under the lamp" (**plant**); copies send ink (**plant**).
- **Relationships:** first real quarrel; Tavi assigned as guide and pretends he's annoyed.
- **Hook:** before dawn a grey-coated Chainman is waiting at the bridge gate.

**Ch5. The Toll Token.** *Bellbridge · Sat 5:45–7:30 a.m. · Left: 57 h 15 m*
- **Event:** a bridge of homes and 26 bells. Tavi boasts about "Edie Marrow's map" and Chainman Pell hears. Grandma Tull hands them a stamped "toll token" of numbers. The toll board lists the bells: 1 Anchor, 2 Barley, 3 Candle... The crew decode FOLLOW BEES and HIDE MAP.
- **Clue / payoff:** **code puzzle** (key in the text; readers can solve first); the full Closure Schedule; Pell flashes a signal-mirror east.
- **Relationships:** Tavi's shame and Mara's anger; Bea spotted the token wasn't a toll.
- **Hook:** the Surveyor now knows four children carry Edie's map.

**Ch6. The Whispering Canopy.** *Whispering Canopy · Sat 7:30–11:30 a.m. · Left: 55 h 30 m*
- **Event:** sound bends here, so voices come from the wrong side. The map's Canopy section is water-stained. Tavi bluffs the way and wastes a lantern mark in shade. They go off-path and their own specks vanish. Bea has seen bees flying straight but says nothing for ninety minutes, then finally does. They follow the bees to Listeners' Hollow.
- **Clue / payoff:** off-path means invisible (**weakness clue**, now personal); **observation puzzle**.
- **Relationships:** Bea's first spoken win; Tavi's bluff exposed.
- **Hook:** from the Hollow lookout, smoke rises over Glasswater: Chainmen are burning reed paths.

**Ch7. The Wanderer's Gap.** *Listeners' Hollow · Sat 11:30 a.m.–1:30 p.m. · Left: 51 h 30 m*
- **Event:** honey bread, slates and hand-signs with Moss Haverly. The map shows the Glasswater Causeway fade (12–1 p.m.), the erasure rule seen first-hand. Tavi tells the tale of the First Chartmaker who left one road undrawn "so the Fold could breathe", and the custom of leaving a gap in garden walls. Moss: "He never cared about reeds before. Why burn them now?"
- **Clue / payoff:** **weakness clues** (the wanderer's gap echoes Nan's saying; the reed-burning).
- **Relationships:** Bea and Tavi bond over being in-between.
- **Hook:** the only way left to the Orchard is the Ropewalk, closing at 4, and a Chainman is guarding it.

**Ch8. The Ropewalk.** *Ropewalk Station to the Orchard · Sat 1:30–3:30 p.m. · Left: 49 h 30 m*
- **Event:** Tavi throws his voice through the Canopy to lure the guard. Idris rushes the basket brake and snaps the pin halfway over the Slack, then mends it with thread. They arrive at 3:15. The Ropewalk fades behind them, leaving them stranded.
- **Clue / payoff:** they were specks on a recorded way, so the Surveyor saw them cross; a wonder view of the hanging Orchard.
- **Relationships:** Idris's first admitted error; Mara realises her detour has trapped them.
- **Hook:** the Ropewalk's last grey strand vanishes, and the only way off is the Chainway, straight to the Tower.

**PART TWO: THE BRIDGE OF BELLS**

**Ch9. The Underside.** *Upside-Down Orchard · Sat 3:30–7:00 p.m. · Left: 47 h 30 m*
- **Event:** the gravity flip at the rim: you step over the edge and stand on the underside, sky beneath your feet. Pickers in nets; "tumbler" fruit. Amma Oduya, half-persuaded by an eternal harvest, confirms Edie and Ansel trained at the Flooded Observatory under Chartmaker Hesper, so "behind the stars" means the dome. Below, Mara sees **nine old posts**, "Hesper's Pier, never finished, never walked." Bea notes cormorants roosting on them.
- **Clue / payoff:** **Old Piles plant** (matches the front-map dots); **social puzzle** begins.
- **Relationships:** Mara's guilt over the detour; Idris generous about it.
- **Hook:** Chainmen's lanterns are coming along the Chainway, and Oduya was told to hold them.

**Ch10. The Drop.** *Orchard underside to open Slack · Sat 7:00–10:30 p.m. · Left: 44 h*
- **Event:** **social puzzle**. Bea notices Oduya's own grandchildren packing to leave for the Market, and Tavi reads her Market trade-ribbon. They understand her fear: losing her young people to change. Mara offers her the truth, that stillness would keep them but they'd never choose anything again. Oduya lets them down "the Drop" basket. Captain Wim's fruit boat crosses the dark Slack and their specks vanish.
- **Clue / payoff:** proof that unrecorded travel is invisible; the Hush explained by Wim.
- **Relationships:** Tavi tense as Market lights appear.
- **Hook:** the Night Market, and Tavi's mother waiting with her arms folded.

**Ch11. The Night Market.** *Night Market · Sat 10:30 p.m.–Sun 8:00 a.m. · Left: 40 h 30 m*
- **Event:** lantern dumplings, haggling rules and hammocks. Saskia wants them gone. Joss refills Edie's pen and explains: **"Ink only sets on a true line. That's why we're never on his Chart: we never stay where we're drawn."** He also warns that copy ink reports. Tavi learns the Scatter Rule (if scattered, regather at Gull Stack, noon next day). In the morning the crew vote: Idris and Bea want to go home for adult help; Mara agrees as "the sensible plan"; Tavi is outvoted. Wim will drop them at Bellbridge before it fades.
- **Clue / payoff:** the ink rule (**weakness clue and climax key**); the Scatter Rule (**plant**).
- **Relationships:** Tavi's hurt at his parents; Bea's homesickness.
- **Hook:** at the marsh mouth, Bea lowers her binoculars: "There are children on that island. And it's sinking."

**Ch12. The Last Toll.** *Glasswater Marsh, within sight of Bellbridge · Sun 8:00 a.m.–12:30 p.m. · Left: 31 h*
- **Event:** **point of no return.** The Glassers' floating reed-mat home on Heron Holm was burned and is sinking. Wim won't take his boat into the shallows. The crew could still reach Bellbridge in time, or take Wim's coracle into the marsh. **They choose the rescue.** **Glasswater puzzle:** the mirror-water reflects the floating Orchard as a false island and hides channels. Bea reads the water-boatmen (only on water) and the heron's feet (only on firm reed). Mara maps it, Idris rigs the rope and mends a split punt pole with thread, and Tavi poles. Ezra, Pim and others are saved. From 11 to 12 they watch Bellbridge fade, the bells dropping one by one into the Slack. Lantern Quay and the way home are gone.
- **Clue / payoff:** Bellbridge (set up in Ch3–5) is lost for good.
- **Relationships:** shared grief; nobody blames anyone.
- **Hook:** Mara unfolds the map, takes out her pencil and draws a circle round the Flooded Observatory.

**PART THREE: UNDER THE WATER, BEHIND THE STARS**

**Ch13. The Reed Ways.** *Glasswater Marsh · Sun 12:30–5:00 p.m. · Left: 26 h 30 m*
- **Event:** a quiet aftermath; Tavi's joke falls flat, then he says plainly he's going on. They commit: no longer escaping but stopping him. Ezra explains that Chainmen began burning reeds this summer, so the Surveyor fears ways he can't draw. The Glassers guide them along chiming glass-reed paths, avoiding the recorded Pole Line ("stay off his roads").
- **Clue / payoff:** **weakness clue** (he hunts the undrawn); the crew now *use* invisibility deliberately.
- **Relationships:** Idris teaches Pim knots; Mara listens to Ezra's slower route instead of her faster one.
- **Hook:** the dome of the Flooded Observatory rises from the lagoon with a light in its one dry window.

**Ch14. The Hermit of the Dome.** *Flooded Observatory · Sun 5:00–10:00 p.m. · Left: 22 h*
- **Event:** Sorrel Penn, a former Chainman, is grumpy and frightened and refuses to help. Supper of pickled samphire and ship's biscuits. He tells them about Ansel and **Pip**, that Ansel "locks with words, never keys," and that the lower dome is reachable only at low breath (1 a.m.) via the old float-lift.
- **Clue / payoff:** word-locks (**plant** for Ch22); the motive revealed in part.
- **Relationships:** Bea wins Penn over by naming his birds; Mara recognises herself in Ansel.
- **Hook:** at 12:30 a.m. the water begins to drop.

**Ch15. Behind the Stars.** *Observatory lower dome · Mon 12:30–2:30 a.m. · Left: 14 h 30 m*
- **Event:** **mechanical and timing puzzle.** Idris works out the float-lift's counterweights, Bea times the Hush, and Mara sees that the star-chart wall is the Fold map reversed. Pressing the "stars" of the Lamp Road reveals the workroom. They find **Hesper's Rule** and the **torn corner** of Mara's map. Mending it (80 cm of thread) restores the legend and Edie's note: "A way is a way only when someone walks it."
- **Clue / payoff:** **the full rule**; the "living way" definition.
- **Relationships:** joy; four children laughing in a drowned room.
- **Hook:** the water returns faster than Bea predicted. They must leave, and they now know what they need: a living way he hasn't drawn.

**Ch16. A Line to Hold.** *Observatory lagoon · Mon 2:30–6:30 a.m. · Left: 12 h 30 m*
- **Event:** **painful mistake.** The plan is a Glasser reed-raft way staked across the lagoon to Heron Holm, kept hidden till 3 p.m. Mara fears it will drift with Pim on it. Misreading "he only holds what *he* has drawn", she inks it with Edie's pen. **It sets.** Within minutes it is erased. As it fades with the rising breath, the rafts tear apart and Pim goes in. The crew get him out, but the Glassers' rafts and harvest are lost and the pen is empty.
- **Clue / payoff:** the weakness boundary is proven: anything on the Chart is his.
- **Relationships:** Bea had objected (quietly) and was overruled; Idris backed Mara.
- **Hook:** Chainmen's boats appear at the lagoon mouth on the high breath.

**Ch17. What Pip Said.** *Observatory to the marsh · Mon 6:30–9:00 a.m. · Left: 8 h 30 m*
- **Event:** Penn stays behind to delay the Chainmen and is taken. Fleeing, the Glassers' coracle splits and Idris mends it (40 cm; 20 cm left). Bea's binoculars go into the Slack and the map is smudged. In a reed hide, the echo lantern on Ansel's old compass (taken from the workroom) gives a boy's voice: "Ansel! A new island! Come on, it's moving!" **Revelation that changes the plan:** Penn's last shout told them the Surveyor will sweep the Long Glass all day and draw-and-wipe any way he sees. Any way in open view is doomed.
- **Clue / payoff:** the Long Glass; Pip.
- **Relationships:** Mara can't look at anyone.
- **Hook:** six hours left, no rafts, no Market, no pen, and Mara holds the map out to Idris: "You keep it. I can't be trusted with it."

**PART FOUR: THE WAY THAT MOVES**

**Ch18. Low Breath.** *Glassers' reed hide · Mon 9:00–10:45 a.m. · Left: 6 h*
- **Event:** **the lowest point.** The last flapjack. Each child breaks: Idris says "I can't fix this", Tavi admits he's never been past Bellbridge and is terrified of open Slack, and Mara confesses her secret wish that Nan could stay exactly as she is. Bea, loudly for the first time, refuses to let them quit, and makes them list everything they know (a fair recap for readers).
- **Clue / payoff:** the list gathers every clue.
- **Relationships:** the crew becomes a crew.
- **Hook:** Mara repeats Ro's proverb under her breath, "darkest under the lamp", and stops.

**Ch19. Darkest Under the Lamp.** *Reed hide to Gull Stack · Mon 10:45 a.m.–12:15 p.m. · Left: 4 h 15 m*
- **Event:** the new plan, built only from earlier pieces. The Long Glass can't see straight down. The Old Piles run beneath the Orchard to the Tower's foot. The cormorants mean the posts are firm. Walking the Piles makes them a way. A way that keeps moving can't be inked. The Hush runs 12–2. They need the Market's rope and boats, and someone in the Tower as lookout. Mara writes the torch code (1 flash: he's drawing north, swing south; 2: swing north; 3: he's stopped). Tavi uses the Scatter Rule to lead them to Gull Stack.
- **Clue / payoff:** readers can assemble the plan a beat ahead.
- **Relationships:** Mara asks others to find holes in her plan and listens.
- **Hook:** the Market is regathering, and Saskia sees Mara first.

**Ch20. The Market Mother.** *Gull Stack · Mon 12:15–1:15 p.m. · Left: 2 h 45 m*
- **Event:** **social puzzle 2.** Saskia blames Mara, fairly. Mara apologises without excuses. Tavi understands his mother's real fear (the Market's children on open Slack) and offers what she needs: only grown sailors, and the children stay at Gull Stack. He also points out that if the Stilling closes, the Market is stilled scattered and never regathers. Mara gives Saskia the map as a pledge, letting go of control. Joss brings 400 m of Market line.
- **Clue / payoff:** the alliance is earned.
- **Relationships:** Tavi and his mother; Mara's growth made visible.
- **Hook:** boats slide into the shadow beneath the Orchard just as the Chainway begins to grey.

**PART FIVE: THE LAST STROKE**

**Ch21. Hesper's Pier.** *Old Piles · Mon 1:15–2:15 p.m. · Left: 1 h 45 m*
- **Event:** in the Hush, Idris rigs a slip-and-swing line post to post (loops that can be flipped from one side of each post to the other), with the blue rope as the first span from the Drop. The children **walk the Piles** end to end, so it is now a living way. At 2:00 the Chainway is gone and the Orchard starts to drift, straining the line. Tavi, on Wim's boat at the Orchard end, holds it. Mara climbs the Tower's service stair alone.
- **Clue / payoff:** every child does their job; Idris gets rope burn.
- **Hook:** the map-room door has no keyhole.

**Ch22. The Great Chart.** *The Surveyor's Tower · Mon 2:15–2:45 p.m. · Left: 45 m*
- **Event:** the echo lantern on the door handle gives Ansel's voice: "Pip." The door opens. Inside is the Great Chart, with the border almost closed, and the Surveyor, who thought Edie still lived in Wrenmouth. Mara tells him she died, kindly. He realises she came by a way he hasn't drawn, sweeps the Long Glass, finds nothing, and deduces "under the lamp". From the gallery he sees the line and starts to ink it. Mara flashes the torch, the way swings, and his ink beads and runs.
- **Clue / payoff:** the ink rule (Ch11, Ch15) lands; the word-lock (Ch14) pays off.
- **Hook:** he sets down his pen and calls quietly for Chainman Pell and the shears.

**Ch23. The Line That Would Not Meet.** *Tower gallery and the Piles · Mon 2:45–3:00 p.m. · Left: 15 m*
- **Event:** the Surveyor closes the gallery shutters, so Mara's signals are cut off, and makes his offer: "In the stillness, your grandmother will never forget you." Mara refuses. Below, Pell cuts the blue rope. Idris mends it with the **last 20 cm of thread** as Tavi and Bea hold the ends. Bea calls the swings herself, then speaks to Pell about his gran's bells, and Pell lowers the shears. The breath returns and the line holds. At 3:00 the last notch strokes towards its start and **does not meet.** The border cracks and the Chart's ink goes dull. It's just paper.
- **Clue / payoff:** everything converges: rope, torch, thread, Bea's voice, Mara's refusal.
- **Hook:** the Fold breathes out, every island shifts a hand's width, and nobody is still.

**Ch24. Gaps for the Wanderers.** *Tower → Rim Road → Lantern Quay → Wrenmouth · Mon 3:00 p.m.–Fri 9:40 p.m. (Wrenmouth), then weeks later*
- **Event:** aftermath. Penn is freed and the Surveyor looks out of a window instead of at his Chart. Pell drives the sail-cart along the Rim Road. Saskia returns the map. Farewell to Tavi, who gives Mara the lantern (two marks left). On the Lamp Road home, Wrenmouth's fog is lifting. On Gull Head: torches, Dad, Funmi, Kasia and Jo. Trouble and consequences. Nan believes her. Weeks later, Mara uses one lantern mark on Nan's music box (young Nan and Edie laughing), and they draw a new map together in pencil, leaving gaps.
- **Lasting costs:** Bellbridge is gone; the Market is no longer secret; Mara is grounded until Christmas; she carries three days nobody else lived.
- **Final hint:** on a winter evening the Gull Head lamp blinks long-long-short, Tavi's "come and see".
