## The Crew's Equipment

*Every good expedition needs the right kit, and needs to know what that kit can't do.*

**The Living Map**
What it does: Shows every road on the Surveyor's Chart, and tiny moving lights for anyone travelling along them. With both lamps lit, it opens Edie's door onto the Lamp Road.
Its limit: It only shows roads that have been drawn on the Chart. Anything off the road is invisible. One corner was torn off and one part is water-stained.
Watch out: Tide-ink drawn on it goes straight to the Surveyor.

**The Echo Lantern**
What it does: Held close to an object in the dark, it plays back the loudest sound that object remembers, for about ten seconds.
Its limit: It only works in proper darkness, and only once for each object.
Its cost: Each echo burns one of the six marks on its candle.

**The Repair Thread**
What it does: Wind it round a break in something that isn't alive, and the break mends.
Its limit: It can't mend living things, and it can't replace pieces that are missing. *Mends what's broken. Not what's lost.*
Its cost: The thread is used up as it mends, about a hand-span for every finger-length of break. There were 200 centimetres to start with.

**Edie's Pen**
What it does: Draws in tide-ink.
Its limit: Tide-ink only sticks to a road drawn exactly where it really is.
Watch out: Whatever you draw, he gets.

**Ordinary Kit**
A big yellow torch. Twenty metres of blue rope. A spiral sketchbook and pencils. A ruler. Binoculars. A tool roll and a spanner. A flapjack tin. Water bottles.
*Ordinary things matter. In the end, a rope, a torch and a few centimetres of thread saved the Fold.*

## Puzzle 2: The Route Planner

It is **9 o'clock on Saturday morning**. You are standing at **Clapper's Landing**, at the east end of Bellbridge. You need to reach the **Flooded Observatory**, and you may only travel on roads that are on the Surveyor's Chart.

**Roads and travel times**
- Clapper's Landing to Listeners' Hollow (Canopy path): 1½ hours
- Listeners' Hollow to Ropewalk Station: ½ hour
- Ropewalk Station to the Upside-Down Orchard (the Ropewalk): ½ hour
- Clapper's Landing to Heron Holm (Glasswater Causeway): 2 hours
- Heron Holm to the Flooded Observatory (Marsh Pole Line): 2 hours

**The Surveyor's Closure Schedule**
- Glasswater Causeway: gone at 1 o'clock on Saturday (fades from 12)
- The Ropewalk: gone at 4 o'clock on Saturday (fades from 3)
- Marsh Pole Line: gone at 6 o'clock on Sunday (fades from 5)

*Do not be on a road while it is fading.*

**Questions**
1. Which way should you go?
2. What time will you arrive at the Observatory?
3. Could you still do it if you set off at 11 o'clock instead?

## Design Your Own Island

The Fold is always changing. Islands drift in from the mist, and new ones are found every year. Now it's your turn.

Draw your island on a page of its own, then answer these questions:

1. **What is your island called?**
2. **What is it like?** Is it a forest, a mountain, a town, a garden, something upside down, or something nobody has ever seen?
3. **Who lives there?** What do they do all day?
4. **What do people eat?** Invent one food, and say what it tastes like.
5. **What is the custom?** Every island in the Fold has a rule visitors must follow, like ringing a bell or paying a compliment before you bargain. What's yours?
6. **How do you get there?** A bridge, a boat, a cable, a road of light, or something else? Draw the way on your map.
7. **Where is the wanderer's gap?** Every good map leaves one.

*Remember: draw your roads in pencil. You can always change them.*

## Answers

**Puzzle 1:** 2 = B, 5 = E, 12 = L, 12 = L, 19 = S … The message is **BELLS RING FOR HOME.**

**Puzzle 2:**
1. Take the **Glasswater Causeway** to Heron Holm, then the **Marsh Pole Line** to the Observatory.
2. You leave at 9 and reach Heron Holm at 11, safely before the causeway starts to fade at 12. Then the Pole Line takes two hours, so you **arrive at 1 o'clock**.
3. **No.** If you set off at 11, you'd still be on the Glasswater Causeway between 12 and 1, while it was fading. *(This is the route Idris wanted to take in Chapter 4. Was Mara right to go to the Orchard first?)*
