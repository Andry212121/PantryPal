## Synopsis

**THE MAP THAT FORGOT HOME** · Illustrated middle-grade adventure · 24,000 words · Ages 8–12

MARA ELLERY (10) lives above her dad's chip shop in the seaside town of Wrenmouth, where she draws sticky-note maps to help her NAN, who is losing her memory. While helping clear the old lighthouse at half-term, Mara finds a map left by the former keeper, EDIE MARROW. As she watches, it fills with unknown islands, a border of 72 marks begins to fade one per hour, and the road out of town vanishes into fog. Nan helps her read Edie's hidden note: a man called the Surveyor has begun the Stilling, and when the last mark fades, everything on his Chart will be still for ever. Wrenmouth is now on it. With IDRIS (11), who impulsively relit the lighthouse lamp, and the quiet birdwatcher BEA (10), Mara steps through the lamp-room door onto a road of light.

In the Fold, a floating archipelago, they meet TAVI (11), a lamplighter's apprentice and storyteller, and his aunt RO, who must stay to keep the lamp burning that holds their road home open. Edie's recorded voice gives a clue: the answer lies "under the water, behind the stars". The Surveyor is erasing roads on a published schedule, and the crew learn that he can only see and control roads drawn on his Chart. Mara insists on a careful detour to be certain, which costs them a day and leaves them stranded on the Upside-Down Orchard. They escape to the Night Market, where Tavi's estranged parents live. Discouraged, the crew vote to go home. But on the way, they spot marsh families stranded on a burning, sinking island. They choose the rescue, and lose Bellbridge, their only way home, as its 26 bells fall into the sea.

Committed now, they reach the Flooded Observatory, where a hermit and former Chainman, SORREL PENN, tells them the Surveyor lost his little brother PIP to a drifting, undrawn road. Hidden behind a star map, they find the founding rule: the Stilling cannot close while any walked road between the islands is missing from the Chart. Desperate to make a safe road, Mara misreads Edie's clue and inks it onto her map. The Surveyor erases it within minutes, a child nearly drowns, and the Market is exposed. At their lowest point, Bea makes them list everything they know. From those clues, Mara builds a new plan. The Surveyor's telescope can't see straight down to the foot of his own Tower. Nine old posts lead there, never walked. And tide-ink won't set on a road that keeps moving. Mara apologises to the Market without excuses, and Tavi wins his mother's help.

In the final hour, the crew string a rope road along the posts and travel it. Mara climbs the Tower alone, opens its word-lock using the echo lantern ("Pip"), and tells the Surveyor that Edie has died. He finds the hidden road, but every line he draws runs off the Chart as Bea's shouted signals keep the rope moving. He sends his Chainman to cut it. Idris mends it with the last of the repair thread, and Bea persuades the Chainman to stop. The Surveyor offers Mara a grandmother who will never forget her; she refuses. At three o'clock, the border fails to meet by a hair's width (a gap for the wanderers), and the Chart becomes ordinary paper.

Bellbridge is gone for ever and the Market is no longer hidden. The children return to a Wrenmouth search party, are grounded, and are believed only by Nan. Mara uses the last echo of the lantern to hear young Nan and Edie laughing. She draws a pencil map of Wrenmouth with Nan, leaving a gap, and draws it again the next day when Nan forgets. On the last evening before Christmas, the lighthouse flashes Tavi's signal: *come and see*.
