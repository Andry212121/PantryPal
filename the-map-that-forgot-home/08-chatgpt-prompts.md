# ChatGPT Image Prompts: Copy and Paste

**How to use these:**
1. Start **one** ChatGPT conversation for all the book's images and upload your approved character sheet first, so it keeps the faces the same.
2. Paste **Prompt 0** first. Then paste one prompt at a time.
3. If a picture comes back wrong, reply with a short fix (for example, "Same picture, but Mara's jacket is tan, not red") rather than starting again.
4. Save each picture with the file name shown.

Inside pictures are **black and white**. The cover and map are **in colour**.

---

## Prompt 0: Set-up (paste once, at the start)

```
I'm illustrating a children's novel called "The Map That Forgot Home" (ages 8–12). Please keep these characters exactly the same in every picture I ask for, matching the character sheet I've uploaded:

- MARA (10, girl): shoulder-length wavy chestnut hair, freckles, tan canvas jacket over a cream top, dark trousers, brown boots, brown backpack, always holding a spiral sketchbook and pencil.
- IDRIS (11, boy): Black British, dark tightly curled hair, brass goggles pushed up on his forehead, blue work overalls with braces, a spanner, a canvas tool roll across his chest, a coil of bright blue rope over one shoulder.
- BEA (10, girl): long straight black hair, a small grey feather behind one ear, brown jacket, binoculars round her neck.
- TAVI (11, boy): curly copper-ginger hair, slouchy blue cap, lots of freckles, chunky brown scarf, carries a long wooden pole with a hook at the end and a small tin lantern hanging from it.

Unless I say "colour", every picture is a BLACK-AND-WHITE book illustration: confident ink lines, soft crosshatching and stippled shading, high contrast, white background, no grey wash, no text or lettering, warm and adventurous rather than scary. Just reply "Ready" for now.
```
